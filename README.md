# Ars Mana HUD

Client-side [Ars Nouveau](https://modrinth.com/mod/ars-nouveau) addon that shows your mana as numbers on the mana bar, and adds a mana cost line to the tooltips of glyphs, spell casters and reactive-enchanted items.

Maintained fork of [Ars NumericHUD](https://github.com/WFPhantom/ArsNumericHUD) by [WFPhantom](https://github.com/WFPhantom), kept up to date with new Ars Nouveau releases. Don't install it together with Ars NumericHUD.

| Minecraft | Loader   | Ars Nouveau |
|-----------|----------|-------------|
| 1.21.1    | NeoForge | 5.6+ (built against 5.13.3) |
